### Inferences
- The SOX multiple has roughly halved, from about 26x (mid-2026) to 13.7x (Nov). It sits about 28% below the cited ~19x 10-year average (derived) and only about 14% above the ~12x historical trough multiple. The market is pricing these as peak-cycle earnings, not as growth earnings.
- The semis discount to the S&P 500 (13.7x vs 17.7x) is unusual for a group with much higher near-term growth. It signals that the market doubts the durability of 2027–28 EPS, especially for memory, more than it doubts current fundamentals.
- The PEGs implied by the fragments are very low. For example, NVDA at ~15–20x forward with FY27 revenue growth far above 50% gives a PEG well below 1. MU at ~4x is effectively below 0.2 on FY27 growth, but that is meaningless if FY28–29 EPS falls as consensus expects.
