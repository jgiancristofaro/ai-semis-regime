## 1. AI financing risk: circular deals, data-center debt, private credit, credit spreads, H2 2026 stress

### Takeaway
AI-linked credit is clearly under stress in H2 2026.
- CoreWeave bonds yield more than 16% and its CDS is above 1,100–1,289bp. Oracle CDS is about 300bp.
- The IMF's Oct 2026 GFSR named an AI correction as a top-three financial stability risk. It tallied about $300B of equity and $650B of debt flowing into weaker AI firms against $1.4T of revenue commitments.
- Nvidia's role as the "central bank of AI" (equity stakes plus guarantees, including a backstop of up to $125B) is now tied to a CEO succession crisis.

### Cited Findings
**Credit spreads on AI borrowers**
- CoreWeave's bond yield topped 16%. Its 5-year CDS reportedly spiked above 1,289bp on Nov 6 (Deutsche Bank data cited by CNBC). Its yields eased somewhat after Q3 earnings — [CNBC, Nov 6, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/coreweave-bond-yield-tops-16percent-as-ai-credit-stress-rises.html)
- CDS levels around Nov 6 ([CNBC, Nov 6, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/coreweave-bond-yield-tops-16percent-as-ai-credit-stress-rises.html)):
  - Nvidia about 139bp
  - Oracle about 298bp
  - CoreWeave about 1,100bp
- Insurance costs since early August, per a Nov 4 report summarized in search results (exact outlet not confirmed) — [search summary of CNBC/Yahoo coverage](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/ais-circular-financing-is-a-feature-not-a-bug-wall-streets-elite-bond-committee-tells-treasury-155811224.html):
  - Oracle and CoreWeave debt protection up about 51%; Amazon up about 79% (from a low base)
  - Broad high-yield up about 25%; investment-grade up about 20%
- The Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee (TBAC) told Treasury that AI "circular financing" is "a feature, not a bug" — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/ais-circular-financing-is-a-feature-not-a-bug-wall-streets-elite-bond-committee-tells-treasury-155811224.html) (date not confirmed; likely late Oct/Nov 2026)

**Nvidia as financier**
- Nvidia has provided liquidity through equity investments and financial guarantees to OpenAI, Anthropic and neoclouds such as CoreWeave. This includes a backstop of up to $125B of a $500B Wall Street credit program — [CNBC, Nov 11, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-jensen-huang-ai-financial-system-risk.html) (via search summary)
- CreditSights expects "creative deal-making and circular financing and equity and credit support" to moderate after Huang's exit — [CNBC, Nov 13, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-jensen-huang-resignation-ai-financing.html)
- Korean press calls Huang's absence a threat to the "circular finance ecosystem" — [Financial News (Korea), Nov 14, 2026](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202611141730429438)

**Debt issuance volumes**
- BofA expects AI-related credit issuance, public plus private, to rise 31% from 2026 to $685B in 2027 — [CNBC, Nov 13, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-jensen-huang-resignation-ai-financing.html) (via search summary)
- Global AI-related debt issuance in 2026 approached about $600B (Goldman Sachs data). Howard Marks cited this in his Nov 11, 2026 memo — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/howard-marks-calls-ai-exposure-170335336.html)

**IMF GFSR, Oct 13, 2026**
- An AI-driven market correction is one of three near-term risks that could abruptly tighten financial conditions. The other two are a sovereign-yield spike and a cyberattack.
- Hyperscalers are about 10% of global IG bond issuance, up from under 1% in 2024.
- 14 disclosed circular deals since 2023: about $300B of hyperscaler equity and $650B of debt went to financially weaker AI firms, which committed about $1.4T in future revenues in return.
- The IMF worries about three sources of leverage: AI-company debt, hedge-fund AI positions, and retail borrowing to buy AI shares.
- Asia looks more exposed than the UK and the euro area.
- Sources: [Quartz](https://qz.com/imf-ai-stock-crash-global-financial-stability-101326); [TechTimes, Oct 14, 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328970/20261014/imf-names-14t-ai-financing-loop-top-trigger-global-market-shock.htm); [ResultSense, Oct 13, 2026](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-10-13-imf-ai-share-correction-global-stability/). I could not reach the IMF document itself.
- On Nov 12 an IMF spokesperson said it was too soon to tell whether AI-share volatility had changed investors' long-term outlook — [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/55989)

**Prime brokers**
- Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan issued extra collateral demands to hedge funds concentrated in AI stocks (FT as cited) — [TechTimes, Jul 29, 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322048/20260729/soxl-slid-63-while-chip-stocks-lost-25-why-recovering-index-wont-fix-it.htm)

### Inferences
- The financing loop is now the most important transmission channel for the bear case. A neocloud or AI-lab funding failure would hit GPU demand directly.
- Nvidia's guarantees create contingent liabilities that tie NVDA's equity to the credit of its own customers.
- The leadership vacuum lowers the odds that Nvidia keeps acting as backstop. That is bearish for marginal demand from neoclouds.
- Even so, the CDS levels for Nvidia (about 139bp) and Oracle (about 300bp) show stress, not distress. CoreWeave is the distressed-looking name.

## 2. Memory cycle: HBM/DRAM pricing, 2027 supply, down-cycle risk

### Takeaway
Industry pricing data still points up, not to a down-cycle.
- TrendForce raised its 2027 blended HBM ASP forecast to +121% YoY (Sep 29, 2026) and expects HBM to stay supply-constrained through 2027.
- Combined server DRAM plus HBM bit supply is expected to grow about 27% in 2027.

Memory equities nonetheless crashed: SK hynix fell about 58% and Samsung about 42% as of Nov 13 (single source). The equity bear case is therefore about positioning, leverage and capex sentiment, not current pricing. The classic down-cycle risk is a 2028 story tied to new fabs.

### Cited Findings
**TrendForce pricing and supply**
- 2027 blended HBM ASP forecast revised up to +121% YoY, driven by supply constraints, the HBM4 mix and the HBM4e ramp in 2H27 — [DRAMeXchange/TrendForce, ~Sep 29, 2026](https://www.dramexchange.com/WeeklyResearch/Post/2/12851.html)
- An earlier estimate put 2027 HBM contract price increases at 70–140% — [TrendForce, Jun 2, 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260602-13074.html)
- Server DRAM plus HBM bit supply expected to grow about 27% in 2027 as new fabs ramp in 2H27 — [DRAMeXchange/TrendForce](https://www.dramexchange.com/WeeklyResearch/Post/2/12851.html)

**Capacity and capex**
- Samsung (Kim Taewoo) expects HBM to take nearly 30% of industry DRAM wafer capacity in 2027, up from about 20% now. HBM and DRAM compete for the same wafers — [TrendForce news, Sep 29, 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/09/29/news-samsung-sees-hbm-taking-nearly-30-of-industry-dram-capacity-in-2027-up-from-20-currently/)
- DRAM and NAND are projected to be 68% of major CSP capex in 2027. This is a sign of memory cost inflation that could squeeze buyers' compute budgets — [TrendForce, Aug 25, 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260825-13198.html)

**Analyst views and the selloff**
- BofA says the memory selloff is overdone and sees strong prices through 2028 — [Seeking Alpha, Oct 2026](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4653899-bofa-says-memory-chip-stock-selloff-looks-overdone); [Ava, Oct 16, 2026](https://ava.fund/news/bofa-says-memory-chip-selloff-overdone-sees-strong-prices-through-2028-16102026)
- Micron says DRAM supply stays constrained through calendar 2028. This comes from a secondary source and is unverified — [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/hbm-dram-ai-memory-demand)
- The July 2026 selloff was partly tied to reports that SK hynix was slowing HBM capacity expansion. Micron fell 13% in one session. Both claims come from blogs only — [Intellectia](https://intellectia.ai/blog/ai-chip-stocks-july-2026-selloff); [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/why-chip-stocks-are-pulling-back-in-july-2026-but-the-ai-cycle-is-far-from-over)
- Through Nov 13, KOSPI fell 42%, Samsung 41.7% and SK hynix 58.3%. This is a single source; the KOSPI figure looks extreme and needs verification — [Seoul Economic Daily, Nov 15, 2026](https://en.sedaily.com/market/2026/11/15/margin-debt-on-samsung-sk-hynix-hits-7-trillion-won-27)

### Inferences
- The memory bear case has two parts:
  - Near term: positioning and leverage unwinds (Korean retail, Situational Awareness).
  - 2028: new fab capacity (Samsung P4/P5, SK hynix Yongin/M15X, Micron Idaho/NY) arrives just as HBM price increases test customer budgets. With memory at a reported 68% of CSP capex, demand destruction or design workarounds become plausible.
- Historically, memory equities peak 2–3 quarters before contract prices. Rising contract prices are therefore not a reliable "all clear."

### Gaps
- No 2026 DRAM spot-versus-contract divergence data found. A falling spot price would be the classic early down-cycle signal.
- No SK hynix-specific 2027 capex number found.
- The 2027 wafer-start additions by vendor are not quantified.

## 3. Power and physical constraints

### Takeaway
Power is a binding constraint. It risks delaying GPU deployments and could shift orders in time rather than destroy them.

### Cited Findings
- Sightline Climate's 2026 outlook counts about 16 GW of planned US data center capacity due in 2026, with only about 5 GW under construction. It expects 30–50% to be delayed. 25% of projects have not disclosed a power strategy — [mgrid.org, Jan 15, 2026](https://mgrid.org/2026/01/15/data-center-grid-delays-50-percent-2026-ai-capacity-risk/); [ZeroHedge summary](https://www.zerohedge.com/technology/half-us-data-centers-are-set-be-canceled-or-delayed-2026). This is secondary; some outlets say 12 GW.
- Bloomberg reporting, as summarized in search results:
  - Shortages of transformers, switchgear and batteries
  - Interconnection queues of 3–7 years in NoVA, Phoenix, Silicon Valley and DFW
  - ERCOT's large-load queue grew from 63 GW to 226 GW in one year
  - AEP Ohio paused new data center interconnections
  - Sources: [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/us-ai-data-center-delays-cancellations-7gw-capacity-crisis-2026/); [ECM Source](https://ecmsource.com/ai-data-center-power-grid-crunch-energy-markets-2026/). These are secondary aggregators; verify against Bloomberg/ERCOT.

### Inferences
- A power-gated buildout extends the cycle but caps near-term unit growth. It also raises the risk of GPU inventory piling up at neoclouds that cannot energize sites, which compounds the CoreWeave-type credit stress.

### Gaps
- I did not find IEA 2026 updates or utility capex data in this pass.
- No quantification of GPUs "sitting in warehouses."

## 4. Geopolitics: Taiwan, export controls, Section 232, China/Huawei

### Takeaway
China is effectively zero for Nvidia data-center sales, so there is limited further downside from China but also no upside optionality. The H200 licensing path is politically contested, and Section 232 chip tariffs are in force in a narrow form. I found no new Taiwan-specific escalation in this search window.

### Cited Findings
**Section 232 and export licensing**
- Proclamation of Jan 14, 2026 under Section 232 imposes a 25% tariff on advanced AI chips imported for re-export to the PRC — [CRS LSB11409](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11409/LSB11409.1.pdf)
- BIS final rule of Jan 15, 2026 moved H200/MI325X license review for China from presumption of denial to case-by-case — [CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11409/LSB11409.1.pdf); [secondary: introl](https://introl.com/blog/bis-export-policy-h200-mi325x-china-case-by-case-2026)

**Nvidia and China demand**
- Nvidia's FY2026 10-K says it was effectively foreclosed from China's data-center compute market at fiscal year-end — [NVIDIA 10-K FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
- Chinese customs reportedly told agents that H200s were not permitted entry. Share lost to Huawei Ascend may not return — [semiconductorsinsight (secondary)](https://semiconductorsinsight.com/us-china-chip-export-controls-h200-2026/)

**Political scrutiny**
- On Nov 12, 2026, Wyden and Neal wrote to Commerce Secretary Lutnick. They challenge the use of Section 232 to collect what they call an "export tax" on Nvidia's China sales and ask Commerce to preserve records — [House Ways & Means Democrats letter, Nov 12, 2026](https://democrats-waysandmeans.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-waysandmeans.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/wyden-neal-nvidia-letter.pdf)

### Inferences
- Policy risk now runs both ways: legal or political challenges to the revenue-share arrangement versus a possible renewed opening.
- The China bear case for Nvidia is largely priced in. For equipment and memory names it is not, because of China's domestic capacity build and its HBM self-sufficiency push.

## 6. Historical analogs after a semis momentum peak and a 25–45% drawdown

### Takeaway
BofA counts four prior downturns (2011, 2015, 2018, 2022) with an average 32% peak-to-trough. The current about 37–38% drawdown already exceeds that.

Recovery times:
- 2018 (about −26 to −30%): roughly 4–6 months to new highs
- 2022 (about −45%): roughly 14 months
- 2024–25 (about −40%, July 2024 to April 2025): a few months, by one secondary source
- 2000–02 is the true bust analog: −82 to −85%, with no durable new high until about 2017–18

What separated bottoms:
- Earnings-estimate cuts arriving (price bottoms before earnings)
- Forward P/E near trough levels (about 12x according to BofA)
- In busts, real demand evidence: falling memory prices and inventory build

### Cited Findings
**BofA's current read (Nov 2026)**
- SOX down 38% in 136 days, against an average 32% in four prior downturns — [BigGo/BofA](https://finance.biggo.com/news/2397bf5b-7780-45ec-9ef8-765e1acf387f)

### Inferences
- Which analog fits depends on whether AI capex actually rolls over in 2027:
  - If hyperscaler capex guides stay up and memory contract prices hold (TrendForce), the 2018/2022/2024-25 pattern applies: a trough within 1–2 quarters and new highs within 4–14 months.
  - If financing breaks (neocloud defaults, OpenAI funding shortfalls) and capex is cut, the 2000–02 telecom/dot-com pattern applies: vendor financing and circular deals were central then too, and drawdowns of 60–80% with multi-year recoveries become possible.
- Signals that would separate the two:
  - Hyperscaler 2027 capex guidance (Q4 calls, Jan/Feb 2027)
  - DRAM spot prices
  - CoreWeave and Oracle credit
  - Size of NVDA estimate revisions
- The 2000 analog fits on financing structure and on the CAPE of about 40 cited by Burry. It fits less well on valuation: semis were at 13.7x forward P/E in Nov 2026 against far higher multiples in 2000, though that figure rests on peak-cycle earnings.
